Revision forecasting and control implementation note (2026-10-09)

Scope and chronology
The revisions respond to criticism of the original Oct6 analysis. They are not preregistered, and repeated Ausgrid testing is not an independent confirmation. Candidate models and settings below are fixed by training/validation before each new test replay. All unfavorable candidates remain in training traces and results. No original code, checkpoints, or results are overwritten. New compute is on D: and per-process numerical thread counts are one.

All-household economics
Use all 299 nonexcluded original households, 244 known and 55 held out, over the original 365 test dates. Identical individual history and GC/PV quality masks yield 108,641 paired household-days: 88,609 known and 20,032 held out. Absolute full operating cost including CL uses 107,713 individual feeder-valid days; missing CL, especially household27, is not filled. Its independent tariff term cancels from paired cost differences, so these days remain legitimate for GC/PV-plus-battery contrasts. Macro household means and micro household-day means are reported separately. The original 220-household actions are reused where the forecast/date inputs are exactly equal; only missing household/date actions are solved anew. All costs are recomputed from the saved float32 actions with one common convention.

Uncertainty
Paired circular seven-calendar-day blocks, 2000 replicates, seed9417; retain all 365 calendar positions and missing values, and jointly resample daily difference sums and their valid household-day denominators. This estimates temporal uncertainty conditional on the sampled households. Replicates are not new test samples. The StoreNet audit uses the same procedure on all 366 date positions, seed918, conditional on its ten households. The three-seed StoreNet contrast first averages same-day seed differences, then resamples calendar blocks.

Low-dimensional and direct-net alternatives
Seasonal12 adds capacity-normalized biases in fixed 06-10,10-14,14-18 clock bands, separately in DJF/MAM/JJA/SON. Net24 adds signed net-load offsets in six four-hour bands by four seasons. Each coefficient is .25*tanh(raw), trained for three epochs with Adam .03, batch32, on the same 2048 known training days as original calibration. Validation uses its original independent 2048 known days and selects shrinkage among0,.25,.5,.75,1. Neither method uses held-out target labels. PV calibration retains nonnegative nighttime-restricted outputs; direct-net outputs are signed net inputs and are not physical PV forecasts.
The direct-net supervised network is 774->128->64->48, ReLU hidden layers and linear signed output normalized by10kW. It receives exactly the original prior-seven-day and calendar inputs. Two AdamW configurations (.001,weight_decay.0001) and (.0003,weight_decay.001), batch512, maximum30 epochs with patience6, are compared on validation MSE. All132200 known training days and44373 validation days are used. The same supervised net model is shared across both tariff calibrations. Do not claim that direct net is empirically stronger when its measured errors/costs are worse.

Load predictor changes
Persistence is the previous complete day's GC vector. The new deployable load MLP is 774->256->128->48, ReLU hidden layers and softplus(beta3) output normalized by10kW, trained with the same full-data supervised configuration grid as direct net. Validation chooses among mixtures of the original HGB and new MLP with MLP weights0,.25,.5,.75,1. The selected weight is.5. Single MLP's inferior validation score is retained. Both frozen original PV signals and separately matched PV MSE/DFL models are evaluated with persistence and the selected stronger mixture. HGB remains the reference. All load-change controls are seed11 ToU.

Matched daily learning and wear
The original physical PV MLP warm-start, original4096 training and1024 validation pairs, batch32, AdamW weight_decay.0001, gradient norm cap1, two rates1e-4/3e-5, four epochs, and epoch0 eligibility are retained. Each MSE and DFL variant selects its own learning rate and checkpoint by validation operating cost, not test error or cost. The old HGB/.02 setting is refitted solely to measure comparable incremental resources; its performance contrasts continue using original frozen predictions. Other matches change exactly the declared load input or wear coefficient. Wear0,.01,.04 are predeclared zero/half/double sensitivities around.02 AUD per AC-throughput kWh; they are not fitted battery aging estimates. For every coefficient, separate (i) repricing unchanged original actions, (ii) reoptimizing with frozen original signals, and (iii) reoptimizing with matched retrained signals.

Continuous-state controller
Use a48-slot day-ahead QP with the original battery, tariffs, and normalized strictly convex tie-break. Remove the daily fixed terminal state and append terminal cost -V(e_end), where V(e)=lambda*e-rho*(e-5)^2, lambda=(minimum buy price+.02)/.95 and rho=.01 AUD/kWh^2. The implementation replaces the old terminal equality by a dummy variable fixed at zero to preserve the existing KKT routine's full-rank49-equality interface; the old shared solver files are unchanged.
Test replay carries the prior planned terminal SOC into the next day, within identical offline GC/PV-clean evaluation segments for all arms. Each new segment starts at5kWh. Future target measurement magnitudes never enter control. QC determines which complete historical episodes are evaluated, not a deployable oracle-based reset rule. Actions remain open-loop within the day. Each day uses its own previous-seven-day features, so the next day's feature history may contain measurements available by then.
Matched training is a two-day differentiable rollout, with each window initially5kWh; the second controller receives the first end state. Keeping original sampled anchors whose next day is also within its respective clean training/validation split leaves4088 training and1021 validation anchors. This is truncated two-day training, not full-year backpropagation. Its loss is [actual two-day operating cost+V(5)-V(final SOC)]/2. Both MSE and DFL use the same anchors and2LRx4epoch budgets, with validation cost checkpoint selection. MSE has two supervised target vectors per anchor. Validation also uses paired two-day replay. Real test evaluation carries state over complete clean segments, rather than imposing a two-day periodic reset.
Report raw settlement-plus-wear and inventory-adjusted costs separately. The full boundary correction is lambda*(Estart-Eend)-rho*((Estart-5)^2-(Eend-5)^2); both linear and quadratic components are retained. It telescopes across each complete segment. Stored segment_id, start/end states, and corrections permit checking this. The older individual training-report limitation string saying 'input-valid run' is imprecise: the executed replay condition is history+target GC/PV clean, identically for all methods.
Twenty actual training household-days, three joint net/SOC directions and three finite-difference scales passed the new controller gradient audit; maximum best-scale absolute error5.32e-10. All directions and scales are saved, including no omitted failing cases. This is local piecewise differentiability, not global smoothness.

Resource interpretation
The timing benchmark uses the identical1024 original validation pairs, twenty warmups, and three repeats, in serial method subprocesses, each single-threaded. Other revision processes were concurrent, so wall times are not an isolated-machine speed claim. Mean process-CPU times are provided as a check. Peak process working set includes interpreter, libraries and loaded arrays, not intrinsic deployment memory. Incremental learning times exclude the common original supervised PV/HGB pretraining. SAA has no iterative task-specific training; residual extraction is timed separately. Both1.5ms deterministic and9.3ms SAA solves are small relative to a day-ahead update interval; speed multiples alone do not establish a deployment bottleneck.

StoreNet
No external fitting, adaptation, scale selection or calendar correction. Retain original source clock and the predeclared2.2kWp reference scale, reporting2.0/2.4 completely. References are not verified household nameplates. Direct gross Consumption and Production are inputs, CL is absent rather than imputed from Australian usage, and the study simulates its own battery instead of replaying Irish battery telemetry. Cost uses original modeled Australian prices, not real Irish bills. Main ToU files were included in the pre-run hash manifest. Six original flat checkpoints were inadvertently absent from that list; a clearly post-run hash/mtime audit and addendum are required, with no backdating of the freeze claim.

Night-support correction to direct-net comparators
After the revision diagnosed negative night net signals in the original direct-net alternatives, a support-matched correction was explicitly adopted. The original signed outputs and all their comparisons remain intact as exploratory results. This is a disclosed post-diagnostic revision, not a new independent confirmation. No foundational supervised direct-net MLP weights were retrained or selected on test results. A fixed projection max(net,0) is applied at the original conservative night slots0:8 and42:48; daytime remains signed. The net_mse_night comparator uses this projection of the existing network. The net_calibrated24_night comparator refits its24 offset coefficients with the projection present after the affine offsets in calibration training, validation and inference, retaining exactly the original2048/2048 pairs,3epochs and shrinkage candidates. Thus the projection is part of calibration learning, while the underlying supervised network itself remains the previously fitted network. Both tariffs select alpha1 on validation. This distinction should not be described as retraining a night-constrained foundational net MLP.
The support-matched primary comparison files are expanded_comparison_tou_support_matched_baselines.json and expanded_comparison_flat_support_matched_baselines.json. On the same108641 household-days, ToU net_mse_night minus continuedMSE is+.03568108 AUD/day; net_calibrated24_night minus continuedMSE is+.02086536. The latter improves on its own projected base by-.01481572 but remains above DFL by+.03217506 [+.02881587,+.03579842]. Flat corresponding differences from continuedMSE are+.01442736 and+.00305844. These results do not establish direct-net superiority. Each of the four new dispatches has1520974 scored night values, with no negative net values. The projection and calibrated forecast formula reconstruct exactly from stored inputs/coefficients; calibration pairs match the original signed experiment and remain inside their chronological train/validation splits.
Final completed Ausgrid standard dispatch audit covers42 artifacts, all PASS; StoreNet36 artifacts are audited separately. Raw solver residuals are in individual dispatch JSON; the independent saved-action audit accounts for float32 storage precision.
