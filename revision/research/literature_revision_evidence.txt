建议嵌入主稿的一段（根据最终结果衔接即可）：
Recent network-aware studies explicitly coordinate distributed batteries or household appliances to provide voltage and balancing services [L36,L37,L39]. These approaches establish that local economic objectives and network requirements need to be considered jointly; they address a different control problem from selecting a forecasting component for an existing household controller. We retain separable private-cost dispatch and evaluate its AC consequences after deployment, while holding the controller and solution-selection rule constant. The resulting comparison concerns when alternative forecasting and learning choices are useful within that architecture, rather than a claim that private-grid conflicts or grid-aware coordination have not previously been studied.
