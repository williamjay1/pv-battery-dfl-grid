Supplementary methods and results

Companion to: When does decision-focused learning add value to residential battery control? Private costs and AC feeder consequences.

S1. Source chronology, coverage and sample formation

The source-clock half-hours and data-quality indicators are retained. Nominal PV capacity is taken from information available before each target day, not estimated by outcome maximization. Known-household training has 132,200 days and validation 44,373; test evaluation has 88,609 known and 20,032 held-out GC/PV-valid days. The CL-valid absolute-cost sample is 107,713 days. Estimated measurements and required-channel gaps are excluded, and no missing meter record is synthesized. Feeder complete dates must satisfy every assigned household’s required channels. The expanded private comparison and joint feeder comparison thus answer different estimands.

S2. Learning and baseline specifications

The 774-input PV network uses 128/64 ReLU hidden units and 48 softplus outputs. Supervised configuration selection precedes three-seed initialization. DFL and continued MSE share 4,096 training pairs, 1,024 validation pairs, batch 32, four epochs, AdamW weight decay 10⁻⁴, gradient clipping at norm 1, and learning-rate candidates 10⁻⁴ and 3×10⁻⁵. Initial checkpoints are eligible; downstream validation cost chooses each arm’s checkpoint. All initialization, sampling and selection records are retained, including unsuccessful settings. Night restriction fixes 00:00–04:00 and 21:00–24:00 to zero. Its initial introduction followed an Ausgrid test diagnostic and is not represented as preregistration.

Three-offset and twelve-offset calibration use bounds 0.25×tanh(parameter) in capacity-normalized units, three epochs of Adam at 0.03 and training batch 32. Calibration uses 2,048 known training and 2,048 validation examples. Validation chooses shrinkage in {0,0.25,0.5,0.75,1}. The seasonal variant crosses three fixed daytime windows with DJF/MAM/JJA/SON. Direct net-load prediction uses signed linear outputs, the same inputs, and full training/validation splits; two prespecified AdamW configurations are selected by validation MSE with at most 30 epochs and patience six. Six four-hour windows by four seasons give 24 cost-calibration parameters. An explicit support-matched nighttime projection and the unrestricted signed diagnostic are retained separately where available; revision chronology is disclosed in the main Methods.

The stronger demand network has 256/128 hidden units and softplus outputs. Validation chooses the mixture of the original HGB and this network among weights 0,0.25,0.5,0.75,1; it selects 0.5. A previous-day profile is the persistence comparator. Residual scenarios use the original frozen forecast backbone and 20 joint load/PV daily residual paths from 147 clean validation dates. A deterministic donor policy supplies household-held-out residuals. Shared battery actions apply in every scenario; no oracle recourse, network coordination or test residual fitting is introduced.

S3. Resource conditions and numerical definitions

Resource timings use 1,024 fixed validation pairs, 20 warmups and three serial method repeats, with one numerical thread per subprocess. Other revision calculations run concurrently. Windows CPU-clock granularity affects individual process-CPU measurements; both wall and CPU summaries are retained. Common original supervised fitting is excluded. Continued-MSE/DFL resource refits reproduce their original fitting protocol, while performance uses the original frozen models. All sample counts, validation procedures and memory scope are reported rather than treating different fitting procedures as equal-budget attribution tests.

S11. Numerical and independent consistency checks

Original strictly convex battery-layer checks include primal state and energy balance, KKT differentiation, action selection under alternative regularization, independent settlement, and an equal-buy/sell negative control. The final revision adds independent measured-cost recomputation on expanded dispatch, a real-data continuous-SOC gradient audit, inventory-boundary reconciliation and per-element export-curtailment/lost-revenue verification. AC local sensitivities are checked against full nonlinear replay and halved perturbations. These checks distinguish model implementation from field fidelity; no solver residual is treated as evidence about unknown household wiring, impedances, phase assignments, or inverter response.

State and settlement checks passed for all 42 completed Ausgrid revision dispatch files and 36 external dispatch files. External recomputation had zero stored-cost discrepancy; the largest rounded-state bound and cyclic-end discrepancies were 1.43e-06 and 1.91e-06 kWh. An additional independent settlement implementation recomputed all 18 expanded comparison files directly from observed load, generation and saved charge/discharge actions, with no failed or unfinished file in that comparison.

The network revision contains 133 completed scenario results: 130 new nonlinear AC replays and 3 original geographic replays restricted to the new common-date mask. All new replays converged, and the maximum absolute active-power balance residual was 1.08e-05 kW. Numerical convergence does not imply satisfaction of voltage or equipment constraints. Export checks include curtailed generation and foregone export revenue.

The local reproduction package contains source-data acquisition records, model weights, derived inputs, saved dispatch, numerical audits and the code required to reconstruct the reported tables and figures. It supports independent cost, observation-mask and checkpoint checks. Fresh training from source data additionally requires the documented public-data acquisition and cache-generation steps. Source-data and software licenses are recorded with the corresponding materials; the package is a local research artifact and has not been assigned a public DOI.
