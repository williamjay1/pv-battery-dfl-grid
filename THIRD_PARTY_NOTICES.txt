THIRD PARTY SOURCES, LICENCES AND ATTRIBUTION

1. Ausgrid Solar Home Electricity Data (2010-2013)
   Publisher : Ausgrid, distributed through data.gov.au
   URL       : https://data.gov.au/data/dataset/nsw-solar-home-electricty-data
   Schema    : UNSW Centre for Energy and Environmental Markets, DARTH Ausgrid 300
   Use here  : processed half-hourly household consumption and PV panel used for all
               private-cost and feeder comparisons. The processed derivative is included
               under datasets/. The raw download is not redistributed.

2. IEEE European Low Voltage Test Feeder
   Publisher : IEEE Power & Energy Society, Distribution System Analysis Subcommittee
   URL       : https://ewh.ieee.org/soc/pes/dsacom/testfeeders/
   Use here  : unbalanced 55-location low-voltage network for the principal AC replay.

3. SimBench benchmark systems

   Licence   : BSD 3-Clause
